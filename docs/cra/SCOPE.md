# Scope assessment

## Declared facts

The maintainer describes an individual's non-commercial MIT project. No paid distribution, monetized service, business support contract or organizational stewardship is declared. The repository account is andreaswiren; no corporate identity, postal address or security email is invented here.

On these facts, SuperOpti is assessed as non-commercial FOSS outside CRA market-supply scope. This depends on actual activity, not the MIT label alone. A natural person can be a manufacturer when acting commercially. The individual described here is not treated as an open-source software steward, a category for legal persons meeting additional conditions. See [Commission open-source guidance](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/cra-open-source) and [Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Article 2 and recitals 18–21](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/2847/oj/eng).

## Reassessment triggers

Review before paid/monetized distribution or services, commercial integration, a legal entity providing sustained support, changed funding/distribution, or remote administration/security-management features. Commercial downstream distributors must assess their own responsibilities.

If brought into scope, classify actual core functionality against Annexes III/IV and applicable implementing rules. Because the app includes network inspection, examine whether network-management or another important-product category applies; do not assume the default conformity route. Record the rationale and obtain appropriate review before commercial distribution.

## Dates and reporting

The CRA entered into force on 10 December 2024. Most obligations apply from 11 December 2027; manufacturer Article 14 reporting began 11 September 2026, already past on this assessment date. These dates do not override scope. [Commission overview](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/cyber-resilience-act).

If manufacturer reporting applies, establish incident ownership and SRP access: early warning within 24 hours, notification within 72 hours, and final reporting as applicable (vulnerability: within 14 days after a corrective measure is available; severe incident: within one month of the 72-hour notification). Steward reporting applies from 11 December 2027. Follow current [Commission reporting guidance](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/cra-reporting) and its ENISA SRP link. GitHub reports do not replace regulatory notifications.
